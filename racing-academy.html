DON'T JUST DRIVE

LEARN TO DRIVE FASTER

Good racing isn't only about speed. It's about understanding the track, controlling the kart, choosing the right line and making better decisions every lap. Torque X driver development can help racers work on the fundamentals that turn experience into improvement.

DRIVER DEVELOPMENT

  1. BRAKING Learn to approach braking zones with greater control and consistency
  2. RACING LINES Understand how your position on the track affects speed through a corner.
  3. CORNERING Build smoother, more controlled cornering technique.
  4. CONSISTENCY Understand positioning, overtaking, defending and decision-making.
  5. LAP-TIME ANALYSIS Use available timing data to identify areas for improvement
